# Shared glossary

Add one complete definition per level-two heading. Keep each entry within 1,200 characters including its heading. Include aliases, context and ambiguities. Organisation-specific definitions require business-owner review.

## Reporting as-of date
Aliases: reporting date, business as-of date.
Meaning: The date to which the cited dataset or metric applies. It may differ from retrieval time and today's date. Use the source's reviewed reporting metadata; do not infer it from this definition.

## LIVE source
Meaning: An actual configured source used under the current user's authorization. This label does not by itself establish completeness, freshness or approved financial semantics.

## MOCK source
Aliases: synthetic, fixture, demonstration data.
Meaning: Explicitly synthetic data used for development and review. It is not a live portfolio, market observation or tradeable quotation, even when displayed beside a live Tableau view.
