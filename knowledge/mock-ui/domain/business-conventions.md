# Business conventions

Confirm organisation-specific terminology with business owners. Ask for clarification when an acronym, segment, reporting period or requested metric has multiple plausible meanings.

Published default filters are retained unless the user requests a supported override. Retrieval time is not a reporting date. A returned summary row is not automatically a deal or unique client. Coupon is not realized return.

Glossary examples and descriptive formulas are not approved calculations. Execute only capabilities allowed by the reviewed source/metric contracts. Every financial figure in an explanation must cite executed evidence; terminology references are separate provenance.
